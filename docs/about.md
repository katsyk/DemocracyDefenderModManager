# About

## Credits & license

Democracy Defender Mod Manager (DDMM) is a derivative work of
**[Helldivers 2 Mod Manager](https://github.com/teutinsa/Helldivers2ModManager)** by **teutinsa**, licensed under
the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/katsyk/DemocracyDefenderModManager/blob/main/LICENSE.txt).

The original project's own site is at [teutinsa.github.io/hd2mm-site](https://teutinsa.github.io/hd2mm-site), and
you can support the original author at [ko-fi.com/teutinsa](https://ko-fi.com/teutinsa).

Changes made in DDMM, on top of the original: provider-neutral mod `Sources` (in place of a Nexus-only model),
folder installs, direct-URL installs, archive path-traversal/symlink extraction hardening, and the rebrand from
Helldivers2ModManager to Democracy Defender Mod Manager.

DDMM is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/katsyk/DemocracyDefenderModManager/blob/main/LICENSE.txt),
the same license as the original project.
